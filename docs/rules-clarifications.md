# Clarifications to the official rules (v1.1)

Answers from the user to questions about `fan-league-rules-bg.md` / `fan-league-rules-en.md`. They add to the official text; where they are more specific, they win. Not served on the site (`.assetsignore`).

## 2026-11-16

1. **When teams and Power Cards come back (§4).** They come back after the half-season break, which differs from league to league. The client running the tournament sets it in the configuration, usually as a fixed date, so when it happens and before which round is up to the configuration. It isn't tied to rounds 13/14. A half-season ends once every team has played every other team, so it has one round fewer than there are teams (14 teams, 13 rounds): you can't run out of teams before the reset.
2. **Goalscorer / Голмайстор (§5).** You can pick a player from either team in the match, not only from the team you predicted to win. A player on the bench counts as taking part: if they don't score (even without coming on), the usual points apply and the card isn't returned.
3. **Cards after the reset (§3.2, §4).** After the reset you always have all 6 cards, whatever happened to the cards of the previous set (used, expired or returned).
4. **Applicable card (§2, §5).** A card can only be added when it is needed: you can't, for example, add Reuse / Повторен избор to a team that is still available.
5. **Variety / Разнообразие (§7).** A correct winner with All or Nothing / Всичко или нищо but the wrong score counts as a correctly predicted win for Variety.
6. **Name.** The official name is **Fens League** (the English file's "Fan League" means Fens League).
7. **All or Nothing / Всичко или нищо with an awarded result (§3.2, §5).** If the match is awarded without being played, the card is returned to the player and the prediction is settled as if no card had been applied (an exact-score prediction: 4 / 2 / 0 pts by the awarded score). If the half-season is already over when the match is awarded, the card can't come back (the reset has given a new set of 6), and the prediction is still settled as if no card had been applied.

8. **Power cards that don't fit the prediction (product decision, 2026-11-16).** Any available card can be added at any time. If the prediction doesn't fit the card, the card stays on but isn't applied, and the player sees "Коз “…” няма да бъде приложен.": Равенство with a non-level exact score; Повторен избор with a winner that isn't a used team (or a draw); Голмайстор with a draw; **Чиста мрежа with a draw or with an exact score where the predicted winner concedes (e.g. 2:1)**; Всичко или нищо in Изход от мача or with a draw. Note: for Чиста мрежа this is stricter than §5, which checks only the real result "regardless of how many goals you predicted".
